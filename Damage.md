# Damage Types

Damage is that from the game this is based on it is very common to not have all types of damage on a weapon. Most but not all weapons have the 3 physical damages impact, puncture, and slash, but there's also the primary elemental damages which are heat, toxin, cold, and electricity. Two primaries typically combine to form secondary elemental damage, heat and toxin is gas, heat and cold is blast, heat and electricity is radiation, toxin and cold is viral, toxin and electricity is corrosive, and cold and electricity are magnetic. Which do the following under the changes I made to the damage system:                              

Impact damage, represented by the symbol 🔨, reduces void armor by 8% per stack, has a max of 10 stacks.

Puncture damage, represented by the symbol, reduces  ferrite, prime, alloy, and adaptive armor by 7% per stack, has a max of 10 stacks🏹

Slash damage, Deals 35% of the damage done as damage per second over 6 seconds, multiple hits of slash in within the 6 seconds adds up to the per second damage, for example a slash of 100 damage will deal 35 damage for second for 12 seconds until I deal 100 slash again 2 seconds latter then I will deal the 100 on top of the 35, then for the reaming 10 seconds I would be doing 70 damage a second. Slash is represented by the symbol 💫  

Heat damage, Deals 50% of the damage done as damage per second over 6 seconds, multiple hits of heat in within the 6 seconds adds up to the per second damage, for example  a heat of 100 damage will deal 50 damage for second for 6 seconds until I deal 100 heat again 2 seconds latter then I will deal the 100 on top of the 50, then for the reaming 4 seconds I would be doing 100 damage a second. Heat is represented by the symbol 🔥,Reduces fossilized armor by 1% per stack.

Cold damage, Staks up to a max of 10 receives +0.1 increased Critical Multiplier on initial proc and +0.05 per subsequent proc to a maximum of +0.5, additive to the critical multiplier. Reduces void armor by 5%per stack. Cold damage is represented by the symbol ❄️

Electric damage, Electric damage, Deals 50% of the damage done as damage per second over 6 seconds, multiple hits of Electric in within the 6 seconds adds up to the per second damage,  for example  Electric of 100 damage will deal 50 damage for second for 6 seconds until I deal 100 heat again 2 seconds latter then I will deal the 100 on top of the 50, then for the reaming 4 seconds I would be doing 100 damage a second. Electric represented by the symbol ⚡

Toxin damage, Toxin damage, Deals 50% of the damage done as damage per second over 6 seconds, multiple hits of Toxin in within the 6 seconds adds up to the per second damage,  for example Toxin of 100 damage will deal 50 damage for second for 6 seconds until I deal 100 heat again 2 seconds latter then I will deal the 100 on top of the 50, then for the reaming 4 seconds It would be doing 100 damage a second. Toxin represented by the symbol ☠️, ignores shields and does damage to the health underneath the shield, unless the enemy has overguard.

Blast damage deals 30% of the damage done after 2 seconds, for example dealing 100 blast damage in the first second then another 100 the next second would result in 60 damage in the 3rd second. Blast damage is represented by the symbol 💥

Corrosive damage, represented by the symbol ⚗️reduces ferrite armor, prime armor, and fossilized armor by 8% per stack of corrosive, corrosive has a max of 10 stacks.

Gas damage lasts for 6 seconds if gas damage is dealt again within the 6 second period the 6 second timer resets, any damage from non gas damage done in that 6 seconds has its status effect triggered and 50% of its damage will be dealt every 6 for the gas’s 6 second timer. For example if I deal 100 gas damage the first second then 100 cold damage the next second the colds effect will be triggered and 50 damage will be dealt every second for the remaining 5 seconds unless gas damage is dealt again within  those 5 seconds then the damage is dealt for another 6 seconds. Gas damage is represented by the symbol ♨️

Magnetic damage, represented by the symbol 🎧, reduces shields by 9% per stack, has a max of 10 stacks.

Radiation damage, represented by the symbol ☢️, reduces alloy armor and adaptive armor by 8% per stack up to

Viral damage, deals 35% of the damage dealt for 6 seconds every second, the damage dealt per second is also add to any other sources of DOT or pre second damage, for example 100 viral damage would deal 35 viral damage per second dealing 100 viral damage again the would would increase the damage to 70 damage every second for the remaining time, if you also did 100 heat damage then the heat damage per second damage would also be increased by the viral damage’s current per second damage of 70, going from 50 damage a second to 120 damage a second for its remaining damage. Viral damage is represented by the symbol ☣️
